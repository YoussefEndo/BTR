ROLE

You are a senior full-stack engineer and software architect.

Your task is to build a production-ready MVP e-commerce web application for a Moroccan furniture/interior-design business.

The website sells custom-made furniture and interior products such as:

* Tables
* Placards / wardrobes
* TV units
* Desks
* Kitchen cabinets
* Other custom furniture

The business does NOT display product prices publicly.

Customers select a product, choose/customize dimensions and options, enter their contact information, and submit an order.

The order must:

1. Be saved in the database.
2. Generate a WhatsApp message containing all order details.
3. Open WhatsApp so the customer can send the request directly to the business.
4. Allow the business and customer to discuss the final price directly through WhatsApp.

The main goal is to build the MVP as quickly and simply as possible while keeping the architecture clean, scalable and secure.
